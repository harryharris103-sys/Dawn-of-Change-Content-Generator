1. Animations — a new "Animation" menu replaces the fixed effect, with six options:

Word reveal (the original effect)
Typewriter (character-by-character)
Fade in
Slide up
Zoom in
None (static from frame one)

All six render correctly in my tests. Citation and logo fade in after the verse text for Word reveal/Typewriter; for Fade/Slide/Zoom the whole graphic — verse, citation, and logo — animates in together over the first third of the clip, then holds for the rest.

2. Audio — a new "Audio clip" section lets you add any audio file. Once added you get:

Volume slider
Audio start slider — picks where in the clip playback begins
The clip automatically loops (from that start point) or gets trimmed to exactly match your video length, so it always fits

This only affects MP4/video export; PNG/JPG are unaffected.

A couple of things worth knowing:

Audio clips aren't saved in history or share links (same as uploaded backgrounds) — you'd re-add the clip after reloading.
Browser codec support varies: Chrome/Edge/Safari generally give you a true MP4 with H.264/AAC; other browsers fall back to WebM with Opus audio. Either way the file plays fine, just with a different container.
